What is Spark?

Spark is a solo role-playing game that hopes to teach compassion and give tools to healthily cope with burnout, apathy, and anxiety. This is demonstrated by simulating burnout-based struggles for a character named Carmen, where the player helps her choose between resting, interacting, or persisting through each challenge. Every choice affects Carmen's Spark, the small flame that makes life feel worth living, and reminds both Carmen and the player that small acts of compassion can change everything.

Spark is a game that takes place throughout the day. So, be ready to move from morning to night.

Who is Carmen?

Carmen is a stellar high school student who is experiencing academic burnout. She pushes herself to achieve great things but without healthy rest, she will fall and lose herself. She needs help, your help.

Who is Sal?

Sal is a fluffy cloud creature who guides Carmen through burnout based struggles by helping her make healthy, appropriate choices. You can be Sal, if you accept this role.

How To Play?

This game is played with a 6-sided die and a standard deck of cards with 4 suits.

There are 2 meters to keep track of throughout the game: Spark and Energy. Spark represents the will to take initiative and live a thriving life. Energy represents the energy to take action in life.

  1. Roll the die once to set your starting Spark level and roll again for Energy levels.
  2. Separate the deck by suit. Each suit represents a time of day.
  3. Separate each suit into 2 sections: A - 5 and 6 - K. Shuffle both separately.
  4. Begin with Clubs (Morning). Draw 1 card from Ace to 5 pile. This is Carmen's struggle.
  5. Draw 3 cards from the 6 - K section of the same suit. These are (your) Sal's possible choices.
  6. Read all three choice cards. Pick the one that fits Carmen's struggle best.
  7. Roll the die. Apply the universal roll outcome to Carmen's Spark and Energy.
  8. If Queen appears in your hand, you may choose the Queen, which allows you to discard all 3 and draw 3 new choice cards. If you do not choose Queen, you must keep what you have.
  9. If King appears and you want to choose it, you must choose an option first. Then you have the chance to roll the die twice and take the better result.
  10. Repeat steps 3 through 8 for Diamonds (Midday), Hearts (Afternoon), and Spades (Night).
  11. At the end of the day, compare your final Spark to your starting Spark. If it is higher, Carmen's day had light in it. You helped her find it.If not, it's okay. Everyone has good days and bad days. The only thing left to do is to do better after.

Card Value Reference:

  • Suit: Club = Time: Morning = Location: Home
  • Suit: Diamond = Time: Midday = Location: Classroom
  • Suit: Heart = Time: Afternoon = Location: Library/Park
  • Suit: Spades = Time: Night = Location: Home

Roll Outcome:

  • 1 - 2: Failed at the choice | Energy doesn't change | Spark -1
  • 3 - 4: Partial success with consequences | Energy +1 | Spark doesn't change
  • 5 - 6: Clean success at the choice | Energy +2 | Spark +1

A partial success means Carmen moved forward but the struggle lingers. Interpret what that means for her in this moment.

If the energy reaches zero, spark decreases by 1 at the end of each suit. If spark reaches zero, the game ends early; Carmen needs rest. That is okay too. Rest is not failure.

How to win?

If Carmen's final Spark is higher than where she started, you win. But if it isn't, that is okay too. Some days are hard. The game ends when the night suit is complete or when Spark reaches zero. Rest is not failure.
